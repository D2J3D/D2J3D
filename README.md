
# 👋 Hello from Denis Gridusov!

## 👨‍💻 Who am I?

My name is **Denis Gridusov**, and I'm an **AI Researcher / Computer Vision Engineer** based in Russia.

Currently, I'm finishing my studies at **ITMO University** (Faculty of Control Systems and Robotics) and working as a Researcher at the **International Laboratory of Embodied Intelligence and Robotics (ITMO)**. 

I have both commercial and research experience. My research focuses on multimodal agents for robotics applications, 3D scene understanding and visual localization. The R&D projects I am involved in focus on building CV solutions for industrial partners.
